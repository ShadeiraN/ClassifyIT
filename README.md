## Lesson: “Think Like AI – Classification Game”
Website Activity

Students use:
ClassifyIT - https://shadeiran.github.io/ClassifyIT/

## 🎯 Learning Objective

Students will:
* Understand how AI classifies data into categories
* Practice identifying patterns and similarities
* Act like an AI model by making classification decisions


## 🧠 Mini Lesson (5–10 min)

Explain in simple terms:
AI doesn’t “think” like humans

It learns by:
* Looking at examples
* Finding patterns
* Sorting things into categories

Example:
AI sees many images of cats 🐱
It learns patterns (ears, whiskers, shape)

Then it classifies new images as “cat” or “not cat”


## 🎮 Activity: Play the Game (15–20 min)
* Step 1: Students Play

Students go to the website and:
Look at items (images, words, or objects)
Group or classify them into categories

* Step 2: Think Like AI
While playing, students should ask:

- What features am I using to group these?
- What makes items “similar”?
- What rules am I following?


## Explain:
When students are playing the game, they are acting like:
👉 A human training an AI model

In real AI systems:
* Humans label data (like students do in the game)
* AI learns patterns from those labels
* Then AI can classify new data automatically


## 💬 Class Discussion 

* What was hard about classifying?
* Did everyone classify the same way?
* What happens if the categories are unclear?


## 🧩 Extension Activity (Optional)

Have students:
Create their own classification game
Give classmates items to sort

Then ask:
Could an AI learn your rules?


## 🧠 Big Idea Wrap-Up

* AI classification = finding patterns + sorting data
* Humans train AI by labeling examples
* The game shows how machine learning starts with human decisions
